# Cyber Security Glossary — 80+ Security Terms Explained in Plain Language

A free, searchable **cyber security glossary** that runs 100% in your browser. **84 essential security terms** — from **2FA, phishing and ransomware** to **zero-day, zero trust and credential stuffing** — each explained in plain language with real-world context. Filter by **Basics, Attacks, Defense, Cryptography, Networking and Privacy**, or search instantly.

## Features

- **84 terms, 6 categories** — Basics (14), Attacks & Threats (19), Defense (16), Cryptography (12), Networking (12), Privacy (11)
- **Instant search** — filter by term or by words inside the definition as you type
- **Category filters** — one-click chips with a live "showing X of 84" counter
- **Plain-language definitions** — written for humans: every definition explains what the term means for *your* safety, not just what it stands for
- **Study-friendly** — vocabulary maps to CompTIA Security+, CEH and CISSP learning paths

## How to Use

1. Type in the search box to find a term instantly, or tap a category chip.
2. New to security? Read Basics → Attacks → Defense in order — a complete mental model in about ten minutes.
3. Everything is on this page: no account, no tracking, works offline once loaded.

## Why a Plain-Language Glossary Matters

Security advice fails when people can't understand it. "Enable MFA and patch the zero-day" is meaningless until someone explains what MFA, patching and zero-days actually are. This glossary exists to close that gap — for students, job-seekers entering IT security, small business owners, and anyone who wants to understand the warnings their devices keep showing them.

## Privacy

The entire glossary is embedded in this single page. Searching and filtering happen locally; nothing is sent anywhere.

This glossary is for **defensive and educational purposes**: learning the language of cyber security so you can protect yourself, your family and your organization — and communicate clearly with the professionals who defend them.
